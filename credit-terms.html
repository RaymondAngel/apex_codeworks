Application Request Terms and Credit Authorization

Read this information before submitting a preliminary request.

Current form: an instructions request

The current form requests instructions and status for a proposed website payment plan. It is not a completed credit application, an offer of credit, a payment authorization, or a contract for financing. No approval is guaranteed. No credit report is requested or obtained through this form. The form is processed through Web3Forms and delivered to our business email; read the Privacy Notice before submitting.

Accuracy and permitted use

Provide accurate business contact information and submit only for a business you are authorized to represent. Do not submit someone else's private financial information, passwords, reports, or identity documents. We use this request to reply about the proposed payment plan and next steps. No application or report may be used for unrelated purposes based on this request.

Separate proposed credit-check authorization

This wording is a review draft, not an authorization accepted by the current form. A finalized credit application must identify the applicant or proposed guarantor, the requested credit transaction, the reporting source, and the authorization date before an authorized credit check can proceed.

Proposed wording: “I authorize Apex Code Works California, through its Credit Review Department, to obtain and review credit information from the consumer or business credit-reporting source identified in my application solely to evaluate my eligibility for the website installment plan I requested. This permission is limited to that application and does not authorize unrelated checks, payments, marketing use, or indefinite future access. I understand that approval is not guaranteed.”

Whether a business report or an individual's consumer report is needed must be established first. The reporting agency's permissible-purpose, access, identity-verification, authorization, and required notice requirements must be met. The current request acknowledgment does not grant this proposed permission. Do not send an authorization or sensitive records by ordinary email.

Proposed dispute-resolution agreement

Read the separate Proposed Mutual Dispute Resolution Agreement. It describes customer-service discussions, arbitration, and court-rights exceptions. It remains a review draft and is not accepted or activated by this request form.

Service and legal limits

Final prices, credit approval, installment obligations, cancellation, default notices, and any suspension or reinstatement terms must be stated in a separate finalized agreement. These pages do not waive rights or remedies that applicable law provides. They do not certify regulatory compliance or guarantee confidentiality against every possible security incident. Contact the business if you have questions before submitting.
