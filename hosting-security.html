Optional Website Care

Practical help after your website launches

Simple options

Website care starting at $150 per month.

Keep your business information current with routine checks, recoverable website backups, and up to one hour of customer-requested content updates per billing month.

Routine checks and backups

Once each month, we check that your website loads and your inquiry form works, and review the recoverable website backup. We also save a recoverable copy before publishing changes. These routine tasks are included separately and do not use your one-hour update allowance. Checks are scheduled; this plan does not provide continuous monitoring.

Your monthly update hour

Use up to 60 minutes across your requests during each billing month to change existing text, replace project photos, or update business hours, phone numbers, and service information. The allowance covers making, testing, and publishing those changes, including edits to existing HTML when needed. You provide the approved text and images.

You do not need to request changes every month. Unused update time expires at the end of the billing month and does not roll over.

Extra work and repairs

New pages, layout redesigns, payment integrations, new features, urgent work, and troubleshooting or repairs beyond the routine checks are quoted separately. If a request would exceed your remaining update time or fall outside the plan, we explain the scope and price and get your approval before starting the extra work. The plan does not include unlimited troubleshooting or repairs.

Requests and response times

We respond to care requests within two business days, Monday through Friday. This is an initial response, not a guaranteed completion time. We agree on a completion schedule for each request. Care is month to month and billed in advance. Email Raymond.Angel.Developer@gmail.com before the next renewal date to cancel future renewals. Your cancellation takes effect at the end of your current paid billing period; it does not require us to approve the cancellation. We continue the agreed care services through that date, then stop the care services and future care charges. Accepted payments for the current care period are nonrefundable, with no proration or credit for unused time, except where a refund is required by applicable law or to correct an erroneous charge. This policy applies only to optional website care, not website-build payments or an installment agreement. Canceling care does not take away or shut down a fully paid website; you arrange ongoing hosting, renewals, and maintenance. Care does not include unlimited edits or 24-hour support. Domain renewals, hosting charges, and other third-party fees are separate.

Google indexing and search visibility

No. Website development, hosting, and website care do not guarantee that Google will crawl, index, keep indexed, or display any page, or give it a particular search position. Being online, submitting a sitemap, or requesting indexing does not guarantee inclusion or ranking. Google controls its own indexing and search results. We provide the agreed website work and care tasks; search-friendly structure does not promise traffic, leads, or sales. Ongoing SEO work and indexing investigations are separately scoped unless expressly included in your proposal.

Google explains these limits in its official guide to how Google Search works and SEO Starter Guide.

Prefer to manage the site yourself or use another developer? The care plan is optional. Without it, you arrange hosting, renewals, maintenance, and future changes.

After full payment, your completed website and the custom code covered by your agreement are yours. Website care is optional. Your domain and hosting accounts stay under your control. Third-party services and licensed assets retain their own terms and fees.
