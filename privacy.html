Information and purpose

Effective November 8, 2026. Apex Code Works California uses business and contact information submitted through its website to respond to inquiries and application-instruction requests, discuss requested services, and maintain necessary business records. Processing and disclosure

Form submissions are sent to Web3Forms and delivered to the business email associated with our Web3Forms access key. Web3Forms, its infrastructure and email providers, our email provider, and our hosting provider may process the information needed to deliver or protect these services. Their practices are governed by their own policies. See Web3Forms privacy information. We do not sell submitted application-request information or use it for unrelated advertising. We may disclose information to necessary service providers, authorized advisers, or as required by law; we cannot promise that information is never shared.

Confidentiality and security

Application-request information is intended for authorized business handling. Limit submissions to the information the form requests. No website, email transmission, or storage system is guaranteed to be completely secure. A form acknowledgment does not establish that sensitive financial information may safely be emailed. Sensitive credit applications and reports are not accepted through this form.

Retention and requests

We retain inquiry information for responding to requests, managing the resulting business relationship, and applicable recordkeeping or dispute needs. Required retention depends on the record and applicable law; there is no single period promised for all information. Credit-report collection remains unavailable pending a defined authorization, access, security, and retention process. To request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted, contact our business contact. We review requests and explain any retention requirements or limits that apply. Deletion cannot always occur immediately where recordkeeping obligations or legal holds apply.

Website services and changes

The site uses browser storage to remember a service selection when available. It also loads Google Fonts and processes requests through its hosting and delivery services, which can receive technical information such as IP addresses. We do not alter our site behavior in response to a browser Do Not Track signal. This notice applies to this website, not third-party sites linked from it. Material changes will be posted here with an updated effective date.
