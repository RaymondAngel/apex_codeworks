CARE COMMITMENT AND CANCELLATION
Care is required during this proposed 12-month package. Standard upfront
website purchases retain optional, month-to-month care.
The final agreement must explain early termination, any continuing website
balance, and whether future care charges stop. Do not assume all unperformed
future care becomes immediately payable.
After the twelve-month package ends, any continued care is $150/month under
separately accepted month-to-month terms. Obtain any required recurring
charge consent and provide an easy cancellation method.
Optional month-to-month care: cancellation stops future renewals and takes
effect at the end of the paid billing period without requiring Apex approval.
Continue the agreed care through that period, then withdraw care services.
No refund or proration of the current care period, except as required by law
or to correct an erroneous charge. This does not determine refunds for builds
or financing. Canceling care alone must not shut down a fully paid website.

PROPOSED MISSED-PAYMENT POLICY — NOT FINALIZED
Requested grace period: 15 calendar days after an unpaid due date.
Give written notice of the missed payment, amount due, grace deadline, and
any permitted suspension before suspension occurs.
Requested remedy: temporary suspension of only the financed site's hosting
that Apex is contractually authorized to control, after the grace period.
Requested reinstatement: unpaid installments plus a $50 reinstatement fee,
only if lawful, reasonable, and expressly agreed.
Do not delete customer data, seize the domain, disclose the debt publicly,
disable unrelated services, or suspend a fully paid website for care arrears.
Retain recoverable site files and define restoration timing, backup retention,
dispute handling, failed-payment corrections, and access handover.
Apex proposes to carry the website installment balance itself. Counsel must
review the financing structure and suspension rights before offering the plan.
Any credit-report service supplies information; approval remains internal.
Document actual restoration costs and have counsel assess the requested $50
fee; a contract label does not establish enforceability.
