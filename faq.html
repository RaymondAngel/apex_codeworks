Frequently Asked Questions

Understand the website package, the project process, and optional care. These are our standard starting terms; your written proposal confirms the scope, final price, schedule, and any agreed differences before work begins.

What to expect

Your questions, answered.

What is included in the $1,750 website package?

Business websites start at $1,750 for up to five pages, a project gallery, an inquiry form, mobile-friendly layouts, and two revision rounds. Final scope and price are confirmed in a written proposal before work starts. Extra pages, payment integrations, copywriting, paid images, and custom features are separate unless expressly included.

How long does a website take?

A straightforward five-page site typically takes 2–4 weeks after the initial payment and required content are received and the scope is approved. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed deadline. We confirm a schedule before starting; delayed materials, feedback, approvals, third-party services, or added work can move the launch date.

What do I need to provide?

Provide your business details, service descriptions, logo if you have one, contact information, and photos you have permission to use. You approve the final text and images. We can help organize your material; substantial copywriting, photography, branding, and paid assets are separately scoped. Please do not put passwords or API secrets in the inquiry form or chatbot; access arrangements are agreed separately.

When do I pay?

For standard website projects, 50% is due after you accept the written proposal to reserve the agreed start, and 50% is due after you approve the completed preview, before public launch and file handover. For a $1,750 project, that is $875 at each stage. Larger or custom projects may use different milestones stated in the proposal. Optional care is billed monthly in advance from its agreed start date.

What counts as a revision round?

A revision round is one consolidated list of feedback on the agreed website, such as wording, photo choices, colors, or layout refinements within the approved scope. Two rounds are included. New pages, a new design direction after approval, integrations, and extra features are changes in scope. We explain any added price and obtain your approval before doing extra work.

Do I keep my website if I decline or cancel care?

Yes. After full payment, you own the completed website and custom code covered by your agreement, whether or not you buy care. Your domain and hosting accounts stay under your control. Third-party services and licensed assets keep their own terms and fees. Without care, you or another developer handle hosting, renewals, maintenance, and future changes.

Are hosting, domains, and other fees included?

Domain registration and renewals, hosting charges, payment-processing fees, paid assets, and other third-party subscriptions are separate from the website build and care fees unless your proposal explicitly includes them. We identify expected services and costs before setup, and you approve paid additions. Provider prices can change; we do not promise free hosting indefinitely.

What does the $150 monthly care plan include?

Optional care starts at $150 per month. Once each month, we check website availability and the inquiry form and review the recoverable website backup. We save a recoverable copy before publishing changes. These routine tasks are separate from the one-hour customer update allowance. Scheduled checks do not provide continuous monitoring, guaranteed uptime, or unlimited repairs.

How does the one hour of updates work?

You get up to 60 minutes of requested changes to existing text, photos, and business information per billing month. Making, testing, and publishing those changes all count toward the allowance. Time is shared across your requests, and unused time does not roll over. You do not have to request updates each month. If work would exceed the remaining allowance, we quote the extra work and obtain approval first.

What is outside the care plan?

New pages, layout redesigns, payment integrations, new features, urgent work, and troubleshooting or repairs beyond routine checks are quoted separately. We explain the scope and price and obtain your approval before starting extra work. The plan does not include unlimited edits, continuous monitoring, 24-hour support, or responsibility for third-party outages.

How quickly will you answer a request?

We respond within two business days, Monday through Friday. That is an initial response, not a guaranteed completion time. We agree on timing for each request based on its scope and current workload. Emergency or same-day service is not included in the standard care plan.

How do I cancel website care?

Care is month to month and billed in advance. Email your cancellation before the next renewal date to prevent the next charge. Care continues through the paid billing period, and unused time does not roll over. Any different notice or refund terms must be stated and agreed in writing before your plan begins. Canceling care does not take away a fully paid website; you arrange ongoing hosting, renewals, and maintenance.

What if I need to pause or cancel a website project?

Tell us as soon as possible so we can review the project status together. Before work starts, the written proposal sets out how completed work, approved third-party costs, unused payments, and any refunds are handled if the project is paused or canceled. A restart may require a new schedule. We do not promise a blanket full refund regardless of work already completed.

What if something does not work after launch?

For the first 30 days after launch, we correct defects in the work delivered under the agreed website scope. New requests, changes made by others, and third-party service problems are outside that correction period. Afterward, troubleshooting and repairs are separately quoted unless included in your written agreement. Let us know what happened and which page is affected.

Can you add Stripe or PayPal payments?

Yes, payment integrations can be discussed as separately quoted additions, using your own eligible provider account. Depending on the project, this may involve hosted checkout or a scoped API integration. The provider controls account approval, supported features, and processing fees. A full online store, subscriptions, or custom payment workflows require their own scope.

Can you build custom software or work automations?

We can discuss custom software programs, work automation, and third-party API connections. These are separate from the standard static website package. Describe your current process and the result you need so we can assess feasibility, scope, dependencies, and price before committing to the work.

Can I edit the website myself or hire another developer?

Yes. After full payment and handover, you can edit the delivered files or hire another developer. Static websites usually require HTML, CSS, or JavaScript knowledge rather than a built-in editing dashboard. A content-management system or custom editor is a separate project. Changes by others may need a review before we resume support.

Will the website guarantee Google rankings or more customers?

No. We build the agreed website with clear content, mobile-friendly layouts, and the search-friendly structure included in your proposal. Google rankings, traffic, sales, accessibility certification, and legal compliance are not guaranteed. Ongoing SEO campaigns, advertising, formal accessibility audits, and specialist legal reviews are separate services and should be scoped explicitly.
